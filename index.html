What we offer

Consultation, diagnosis, and coordinated treatment.

Services are structured around the patient's journey — not around hospital departments.

General & Specialist Consultation

In-person urology consultations covering routine concerns through to complex referrals, led by general practitioners and specialists.

Our approach

Built for a subject patients rarely want to talk about twice.

Urological concerns are sensitive, and often delayed. The practice is structured to reduce friction at every step.

—Discretion by default

Private consultation rooms and confidential handling of every case, from first enquiry to follow-up.

—One consultant, one case

Wherever possible, the same clinician stays with a patient's case through diagnosis and referral.

—Clear, plain-language guidance

Diagnoses and next steps are explained without unnecessary jargon, so patients can make informed decisions.

—Timely referral network

Direct coordination with specialists and surgical partners to reduce the wait between diagnosis and treatment.
